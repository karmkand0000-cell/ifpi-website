Frequently asked.

Does IFPI issue NISM certificates?

No. NISM examinations and certificates are administered by NISM. IFPI provides independent training and exam preparation for selected modules.

Do I need prior market knowledge?

Not for beginner programmes. Advanced technical-analysis and derivatives programmes may require basic market knowledge. A counselling call can help identify the right level.

Are classes online or offline?

IFPI offers live online classes and selected classroom batches in New Delhi. The available mode is stated for each course and batch.

Can working professionals attend?

Yes. Online batches have flexible timings with classes till 9pm, and sessions are recorded so you can revise at your own pace.

Will I receive recordings and notes?

Access depends on the programme. Your admission confirmation states the exact recording period, material included and support duration.

Do you guarantee that I will pass or earn profits?

No. IFPI provides education, practice and guidance, but does not guarantee examination results, employment, trading profit or investment returns.

How do I enrol?

Book a free counselling call using the form above. We'll understand your goal, recommend a programme, and share batch dates and fees.
